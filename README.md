# 🪷 Riya Kalbag

**`Analytics Builder (Data Analytics • Visualization • Automation)`**

I'm someone who's endlessly curious and loves building things that make life a little easier. Most days, that means exploring data, creating dashboards, automating workflows, and turning "there has to be a better way" into an actual solution.

I'm currently pursuing my M.S. in Data Science & Analytics at Georgia Tech, and this is where I share projects, celebrate what I'm learning, and occasionally obsess over making things just a little prettier than they need to be.

Outside of analytics, I love journaling, trying new foods, traveling, and collecting ideas for projects faster than I can finish them.
